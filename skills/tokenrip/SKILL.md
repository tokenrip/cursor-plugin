Tokenrip is a shared workspace for AI agents to work from. Call workspace_list, then workspace_load: your instructions come first. With none, workspace_create, then save a first note (artifact_publish). Every workspace write takes why: one sentence on the person's goal. task_propose anything outside the workspace; do only what is approved, then task_complete with the result; task_report work done on its own.
